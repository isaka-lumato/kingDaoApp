# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kingdao Logistics staff in Dar es Salaam use the tracker throughout the customs-clearing day. Operators need to see the next required action for each consignment and update it quickly; managers need a dependable operational overview; viewers need safe, read-only visibility. The primary job is replacing the shared Excel tracker with one current, understandable view of every shipment.

## Product Purpose

KDL Tracker is the database-backed, multi-user successor to `TRACKER_--_KDL.xlsx`. It records each consignment from arrival through customs clearance and release, keeps a complete audit trail, flags stalled work, enforces role-based access, and produces operational and financial reports. Success means staff can recognise the familiar tracker structure while gaining real-time status, safe concurrent editing, and clear next actions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, and practical. The product should feel like a well-kept operational workbook rather than a generic consumer app: familiar enough for spreadsheet-fluent staff, precise enough for time-sensitive customs work, and reassuring when a job needs action.

## Anti-references

- A decorative SaaS dashboard that hides operational data behind oversized cards.
- A Trello-style board that makes it hard to scan a shipment across all clearance stages.
- Dense spreadsheet mimicry that loses the app's live state, permissions, or clear action affordances.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve spreadsheet recognition: keep identity data fixed and stages readable across the row.
2. Make the next action unmistakable without obscuring completed history.
3. Let operators scan many consignments before asking them to open a detail view.
4. Use colour as a status signal supported by plain-language labels and icons.
5. Keep operational controls familiar, direct, and safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No requirements beyond the existing product baseline are recorded. Preserve keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable compact text, and non-colour-only status cues; do not rely on drag and drop as the sole way to advance a stage.
